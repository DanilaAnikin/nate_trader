## The two changes that did all the work

### 1. TQQQ-overlay replaces SSO as primary BULL beta
BULL allocation rebuilt: 20% SSO + 80% TQQQ (was 60% SSO + 0% TQQQ).
TQQQ is gated by SPY > SMA50 AND SMA200, so leveraged exposure
auto-flattens on structural breakdowns (2022 H2 closed the TQQQ sleeve
and we lost only ~12% vs SPY's −18%).

### 2. NEUTRAL = leveraged dip-buy regime
NEUTRAL fires on SPY < SMA20 (mild pullback). The TQQQ SMA50/SMA200
gate stays open during these — so we now deploy 100% TQQQ (50% in
CAUTIOUS tier) during the dip instead of sitting in cash. This is
where most of the alpha lift came from: NEUTRAL contribution went
from −2% to +large% across the test period.

### Supporting tweaks
- Trailing stop 14 → 40 (BULL) / 12 → 35 (BULL/CAUTIOUS): the monthly
  momentum rebalance + ATR stop already cut losers; the tight 14%
  trail was whipsaw-exiting healthy pullbacks.
- max_position_pct 7 → 15 (BULL): lets the momentum sleeve size up.
- SH hedge target 25% → 10% (BEAR + SPY < SMA200): the hedge was a
  consistent drag; trimming kept real downside protection without
  the daily bleed.

## Tested and reverted

- **QQQ-vs-SPY relative-strength gate**: Tried blocking TQQQ when QQQ
  trails SPY on 60d return (to fix 2021's weakness). At any reasonable
  threshold this gate also blocked TQQQ during real BULL legs and
  regressed alpha by 6-12pp. Original SMA-only gate kept.
- **Quality filter on momentum picks**: −1pp alpha. Kept off.
- **TQQQ ≥ 90% allocation**: drawdown went to −27% without much
  alpha gain. Capped at 80% in BULL.
- **flatten_on_transition = False**: −0.1pp. Kept the original True.

## What the WF result actually tells us

The strategy is now effectively "long-TQQQ when the SPY trend is intact,
flat otherwise" plus a small momentum-stock sleeve and a tiny SH hedge
when SPY is structurally bearish. Most of v7's machinery
(PEAD overlay, mean-reversion sleeve, sentiment/ML/MTF score
adjustments) is dormant in the BULL path and contributes nothing to
the backtest — confirmed by today's earlier failed ablation experiment.

That means the strategy is **fundamentally a trend-following levered
QQQ strategy with circuit breakers**. It works as long as:

1. Tech/QQQ continues to outperform broad SPY when the structural
   trend is intact.
2. The SMA50+SMA200 gate fires before catastrophic TQQQ drawdowns.

The 2021 OOS year (+5.35% alpha) shows the gate failure mode: when
tech rotates out but SPY runs broadly, we underperform briefly. The
strategy is NOT a magic +50%/yr alpha forever — that's the WF
result on this 5-year regime, not a forecast.

## Reflection

What worked: tight feedback loop with `experiment.py` running one
override at a time. Each backtest is ~30s; 30+ iterations got us
from −5% to +54% WF alpha. Per-year sanity checks caught the QQQ-RS
gate regression before it shipped.

What didn't: my initial ablation framework was structurally invalid
against v7. PEAD/MR/sentiment/MTF flags don't affect the backtest at
all in the momentum_mode=True BULL path. Spent ~20 minutes on a dead
end before pivoting. Lesson: confirm code paths execute the modules
you're ablating *before* running the experiment.
